Cryptographic hashes

SHAKE128/256 use fixed 256/512-bit outputs. SHA-1, MD4, and MD5 are legacy and cryptographically broken; do not use them for security, signatures, password storage, or tamper protection.

Non-cryptographic checksums

Fletcher-32 reads big-endian 16-bit words and zero-pads an odd final byte. xxHash32/64 use seed 0. Non-cryptographic checksums detect accidental changes; they are not suitable for protection against malicious tampering.
